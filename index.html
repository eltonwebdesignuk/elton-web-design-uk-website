Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on pricing, timelines, and what’s included. Still unsure? Get a quote or use the chat button in the corner.

How much does a website cost?

Build & ship is £495 one-off. Build, ship & manage is £495 upfront plus £99 per month. The optional AI knowledge chatbot add-on is £495 setup plus cloud usage at cost.

How long does a build take?

A typical new site takes 2–4 weeks from brief to launch, depending on how quickly you provide content and feedback. We can often start within 1–2 weeks of your deposit.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to begin work and 50% upon completion (when your site goes live). Monthly management (if chosen) is billed in advance. Payment is by bank transfer.

What’s included in each package?

Both packages include a tailored multi-page site, mobile-friendly layout, contact form, basic SEO, launch on your domain, and two rounds of revisions. Monthly management adds hosting help, security updates, small content edits, uptime checks, and a short monthly summary.

Do you only work with UK businesses?

We focus on UK local businesses, but we can work with English-speaking clients elsewhere by arrangement. Email hello@eltonwebdesignuk.com to discuss.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Yes — we refresh outdated sites, not only build from scratch. We can replace hard-to-use mobile layouts, old WordPress themes, Wix/Squarespace sites, or a Facebook-only presence.

Do you handle domains and hosting?

We help you launch on your own domain. You own the domain. With monthly management we handle hosting help and updates ongoing. If you leave management, we provide a clear handover.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions on design and content before launch. A revision round means consolidated feedback on the current draft — not unlimited one-line tweaks spread over weeks.

What is the AI chatbot add-on?

A chat agent on your site that answers from your FAQs and policies — not the open internet. £495 one-off setup, plus cloud usage at cost (typically pennies at low volume). Available with either website package.
